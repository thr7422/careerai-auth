Privacy Policy

CareerAI: AI Resume Builder

At CareerAI: AI Resume Builder, accessible from our mobile application, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by CareerAI and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

We collect several types of information to provide and improve our AI resume services:

  • Personal Information: When you sign up, we collect your email address and name (via Supabase Auth or Google Sign-in).
  • Resume Data: Any information you enter to build your resume (work experience, education, skills, contact details, etc.).
  • Media: If you choose to upload an avatar or scan a resume, we process the image/file you provide.
  • Device Information: We may collect basic device info like model and OS version to troubleshoot bugs.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect in the following ways:

  • To provide, operate, and maintain our app.
  • To generate AI-powered resume and cover letter content (using Groq AI services).
  • To save your progress and sync your resumes across devices via our secure cloud storage (Supabase).
  • To show personalized or non-personalized advertisements (via Google AdMob).

3. AI Processing & Third-Party Services

  • Groq AI: To generate professional text, your input data is processed by AI models. We do not use your personal data to "train" public AI models.
  • Supabase: Your account and resume data are stored securely on Supabase servers.
  • Google AdMob: We use AdMob to display ads. Google may use cookies or device identifiers to serve ads based on your interests.
  • Google Play Services: Used for app functionality and analytics.

4. Data Security

We value your trust in providing us your personal information. We use industry-standard encryption to protect your data both during transmission and while stored in the cloud. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

5. Children’s Privacy

CareerAI does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you think that your child provided this kind of information on our app, we strongly encourage you to contact us immediately and we will do our best to promptly remove such information from our records.

6. Your Data Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of the data we have about you.
  • Deletion: You can delete your account and all associated resume data directly from the app or by contacting us via email.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
